About the Artist

Hi, I’m Nhi

I’m a local pottery enthusiast who enjoys transforming simple ideas into meaningful, handmade pieces. My pottery journey is rooted in creativity, curiosity, and a love for making everyday spaces feel more personal. I create functional and decorative ceramics, including mugs, bowls, planters, vases, and one-of-a-kind pieces.

Because each piece is shaped and finished by hand, no two creations are exactly alike. The small variations in form, texture, and glaze are part of what makes handmade pottery distinctive.

My Creative Series

My Customizing My Apartment series began as a personal project to make my living space feel more intentional, creative, and uniquely mine.

Through this series, I design and create pottery and art for different areas of my apartment. Each project begins with a simple question: How can I make this space more functional, beautiful, and personal?

The series may include handmade coasters, dinnerware, lotion dispensers, olive oil bottles, and other creations inspired by the needs of my home. It allows me to combine my love for ceramics and creativity while surrounding myself with meaningful, handmade pieces that I can enjoy and use every day.

More than a decorating project, this series documents my creative process—from the initial idea and clay selection to forming, glazing, firing, and finally placing the finished piece in its new home.

“Creating a home that feels personal, one handmade piece at a time.”
